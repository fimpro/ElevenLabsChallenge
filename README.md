# SightVoiceApp  
🌍 Your Personal Tour Guide  

Welcome to **SightVoiceApp**! Experience sightseeing in a way that's flexible, accessible, and perfectly tailored to your unique interests.

## Why SightVoiceApp?  
Say goodbye to outdated and cumbersome guided tours! **SightVoiceApp** combines personal guidance with the joy of solo exploration.  

### The Problem:
- 🗣 Too generic content in current audio guides  
- 🌎 Limited language options  
- 💀 Dry and unengaging experiences  
- 🛣 Fixed routes with no flexibility  
- ⏰ Time-consuming planning  
- 💰 Expensive guided tours  

### Our Solution:
- 🚶 Explore independently while receiving professional-grade guidance  
- 📜 Customized, engaging storytelling matched to your interests  
- 🎙️ Audio in 6+ languages, with adaptable voices to fit your vibe  
- 📲 Accessible on your smartphone — your tour guide in your pocket!

## Features at a Glance  

### 1. Personalized Audio Guide  
- Natural language input to set up your interests.  
- Tailored audio guides delivered on the go.

- Create and customize a voice guide that suits your vibe!  

### 3. Seamless Language Support  
- Audio in 6+ languages ensures a global reach.  

---  

## How It Works  
1. Choose your guide's voice and language.  
2. Set your areas of interest using a simple natural language input.
3. Start exploring — the app detects your location and provides context-aware audio guides as you approach landmarks.
